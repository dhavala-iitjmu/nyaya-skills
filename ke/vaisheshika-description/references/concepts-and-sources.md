# Vaiśeṣika: conceptual scope and sources

## Historical anchor and textual layers

Vaiśeṣika offers a realist categorical ontology, not a synonym for modern data
modeling. The six categories enumerated in Vaiśeṣika Sūtra 1.1.4 provide this
skill's starting point. Its historically developed analysis of universals,
ultimate particularity and inherence is not fully stated in that single verse.
Later Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika classifications include absence as a seventh category.
Keep the sūtra, later commentaries and modern engineering analogy distinct.

The classical list of substances includes earth, water, fire, air, ether, time,
direction/space, self and mind. This skill does not replace that list with modern
chemical elements or software components. Classical motion, substance and mind
make substantive commitments; an LLM's reasoning policy is not thereby the
historical manas. An application may select useful distinctions without claiming
to adopt the whole metaphysics, atomism or soteriological aim.

## Avoid these category substitutions

- **Particular quality versus universal:** this pot's particular color is not the
  same item as the universal it instantiates, the word naming it, or its measured
  representation. A class of red records is an encoding whose meaning needs an
  explanation, not automatic proof of a classical universal.
- **Ultimate particularity versus identifier:** viśeṣa in the developed account
  concerns ultimate differentiation of eternal substances. A UUID distinguishes
  records under a convention; it is not a historical ultimate individuator. Give
  the task's practical identity criteria without pretending to solve that problem.
- **Inherence versus conjunction:** redness in its bearer and a constituted whole
  in its constitutive parts are standard inherence cases in the developed account.
  A pot touching a table exemplifies a separable relation instead. A strong
  business dependency or `partOf` field alone does not establish samavāya.
- **Motion versus general event:** a pot's movement is different from its color;
  a training job's state transition can be analyzed as an engineering process,
  but is not silently the same theory as the classical category karma.
- **Bearer versus representation:** a record about a pot is distinct from the pot.
  A metric value in a report, the measurement event and the model–evaluation
  configuration are also distinct, even if stored in one database row.

Use resemblance and difference to motivate each distinction, not only to collect
similarities. If a mapping breaks on a material contrast, narrow it, mark it as an
engineering convention, leave it unassigned, or ask for a domain decision.

## Absence without a closed-world shortcut

An absence judgment needs what is absent (counterpositive/pratiyogin), where it is
absent, relevant time/qualification and a warranted means of knowing it. Developed
accounts distinguish prior absence, destruction/subsequent absence, mutual
nonidentity and absolute absence. These categories cannot be read straight from
SQL empty results or administrative deletion events.

For example, a complete, functioning sensor sweep might support a bounded
negative judgment. A failed query or incomplete index does not. A pot not being
on a table now does not by itself justify a timeless metaphysical classification
as absolute absence. Distinguish the historical taxonomy from practical negative
assertions and the observations supporting them.

## Reading map

Primary edition: Nandalal Sinha, *Vaiśeṣika Sūtra with commentaries* (1923).
Separate the aphorisms from later commentarial explanations. Older English terms
such as “species” and “combination” should not be read as contemporary biological
species or arbitrary computational composition.

1. [1.1.4: categories and resemblance/difference](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/vaisheshika-sutra-commentary/d/doc427557.html).
   Primary anchor for the six-category starting point. Its commentary also discusses
   differentiated nonexistence; do not attribute every commentary claim to the sūtra.
2. [Book I contents](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/vaisheshika-sutra-commentary/d/doc427552.html)
   locates 1.1.5–7 on substances, qualities and motion. Their historical enumerations
   constrain what the terms meant; they are not a schema for forcing modern domains.
3. [7.2.26 and commentary: samavāya](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/vaisheshika-sutra-commentary/d/doc485967.html).
   Consult when distinguishing inherence and conjunction and analyzing the relata
   of whole/part, quality/bearer and universal/instance descriptions.
4. [Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Nyāya](https://iep.utm.edu/nyaya/),
   metaphysics sections on substance, quality, action, universals, inherence,
   individuators and absence. This is a scholarly account of the related developed
   Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika conceptual setting, not evidence that every early author held
   identical doctrines.

## Deliverable discipline

Give the minimum useful category analysis with explicit mapping limits. Do not
create atoms, selves, eternal substances or universals in a domain ontology merely
to satisfy a checklist. Explain an unassigned category when it would otherwise be
misleading. Historical reconstruction needs a specified text/tradition and may
require a specialist; engineering utility alone does not establish fidelity.
